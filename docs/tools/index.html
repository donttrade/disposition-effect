Where the numbers come from

Every preview on this page is a tool run on real public data, recomputed by script from files whose hashes we keep. Nothing is invented and nothing was picked for how it looks.

The trade-file tools and the test’s summary
Laramee, R. (2025). Stock Transactions Dataset. Mendeley Data, V1. doi:10.17632/k4tnk2nggv.1. Licensed CC BY 4.0. Changes: we kept the dataset’s market buys and sells (its combined, split-adjusted sheet) and turned them into a trade file, with ticker names replaced with codes; results are price gains in US dollars before fees, FX and dividends. Selection: the whole dataset, one anonymous investor; not picked by result. The dataset’s authors do not endorse donttrade. Why this dataset: It is the only openly licensed, fill-level record of one real account we found. Rule: The whole dataset: every market buy and sell in its combined, split-adjusted sheet, in time order, US dollar rows only. Nothing is picked by result. The Readiness Check: One-phase challenge rules, the first rule set in our library, shown without the firm’s name: $100,000; 10% target; 5% daily loss; 10% maximum loss (static); at least 3 trading days; no time limit stated, so none is applied. The file’s times are read as UTC, because the dataset states no time zone, and a trading day ends at midnight UTC+3, the rule’s own day boundary. The heatmap: The tool names no best or worst cell, so neither does the preview. Hours are as in the file, which states no time zone; hours with no trades are left out.
Lot size and risk-reward
Board of Governors of the Federal Reserve System, H.10 Foreign Exchange Rates, euro, noon buying rates in New York (public domain): federalreserve.gov/releases/h10/hist/dat00_eu.htm. Rule: The calendar month before the month of the H.10 file’s latest rate, so that the month is complete. Shown for scale only: no entry, stop, target or size is taken from it. The month shown: September 2026. The risk-reward preview shows the break-even formula itself, with no example numbers.
Disposition effect
Odean, T. (1998). Are investors reluctant to realize their losses? Journal of Finance 53(5), 1775–1798, Table I, entire year. doi:10.1111/0022-1082.00072. Rule: Odean’s four published counts for the entire year, typed into the calculator. They follow the calculator’s counting rule (days with a sale). Our 76.1% study uses a public CC0 panel (doi:10.7910/DVN/NI6SBJ) and a different counting rule, so its numbers are not shown in the calculator.

No cookies. What this site stores and sends

This site sets no cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics and Umami Cloud, which also counts some button clicks; neither sets a cookie. It stores your light/dark choice in your own browser and, if you buy the Revenge Trading Report, your access key there too. For the report your browser sends our server only stripped trade numbers. The server does not keep them: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first result, to match a re-test to the same history. Your file itself is not uploaded. Card payments are handled by Stripe. Forms send what you type to Web3Forms, which emails it to us, and subscribing opens Substack; their privacy policies apply.
