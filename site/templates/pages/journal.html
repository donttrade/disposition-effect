What it does

Import the trade history your platform already exports. The journal lists every closed trade, puts each day’s result on a P&L calendar, and lets you mark each trade with a setup, tags, mistakes and a note, all in words you choose. Filters and totals show what happened in the trades you pick. Importing the same export twice adds nothing: trades already in the journal are recognised and skipped, and any import can be undone.

A day’s result counts the trades that closed that day, in the time written in your file. Open positions are not counted until they close.

Which files it reads

MetaTrader 5 and 4 history reports (HTML), MetaTrader 5 deals (CSV), IBKR Flex Query trade files, Tradovate order exports and TradingView order history (all in beta), and the donttrade CSV template: one row per fill, or one row per round trip. The same reader as the free trade-stats tool.

Where your journal is kept

The journal saves your trades and notes in this browser, on this device only. They are not uploaded: this page is built so it cannot make network requests (you can check in your browser’s network panel). Clearing this site’s data in your browser deletes the journal, so keep a backup file. We cannot recover it.

Safari deletes a site’s saved data if you don’t open the site for 7 days of browsing. Keep a backup file. On iPhone or iPad, adding the journal to your Home Screen keeps it out of that rule.

Questions

Is the journal really free?

Yes, with no account and no card. It runs on your device, so it costs us nothing to run, and it stays free. A paid add-on, if one ever exists, would be separate and optional.

Where are my trades stored?

In this browser, on this device. The page cannot send them anywhere: its security policy blocks every network request. To move the journal to another computer or browser, save a backup file and restore it there.

What happens if I import the same file twice?

Nothing is added the second time. Each trade gets a key made from what it is (symbol, side, times, size, prices and result), so a trade already in the journal is skipped. A trade with the same symbol, side and times but other numbers is shown as a possible duplicate for you to decide on.

Is a trade counted on the day it opened or closed?

On the day it closed, in the time written in your file. Open positions are not counted.

Does it tell me what to trade?

No. It shows what happened in your own past trades. It does not suggest setups, sizes or trades.
