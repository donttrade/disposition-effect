When you get it
Right after payment, in your browser. One re-test is included, to run 30 to 60 days after your first result. Your re-test runs on the version of the report that is live when you run it; every change is dated on the corrections page. Restrictions
Two runs: the report and one re-test of the same account (the re-test file has to share at least half of the first file's trades). At most 20,000 rows per file. A tested habit with fewer than 30 trades in either group is shown as "not enough trades to tell". If 5% or more of a file's rows open at exactly 00:00 and are held a whole number of days, as in exports with dates but no times, no habit is tested, and the habits that depend on the order of trades within a day (re-entering soon after a loss, size after a loss, trading after a losing run) get no figure.
