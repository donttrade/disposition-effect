Automatic renewal ({{ products.tracker79.name }})

  1. What renews: your {{ products.tracker79.name }} subscription, for a new 12-month period, at the same price and on the same terms, unless we give notice under (e).
  2. When: on each anniversary of your purchase, until you cancel.
  3. Your other choice: you can end the subscription at any time before the renewal date, at no cost, in the billing portal. No further amount is then charged.
  4. Our notices: we email you about 45 days before each renewal, and again about 20 days before. Each notice gives the date and amount of the renewal, what happens if you do nothing, how to cancel online at no cost, and updated pre-purchase information.
  5. Changes to price or terms: we email you 30 to 45 days before the renewal concerned, with the text of the change. A price increase applies only with your express agreement; without it, the subscription ends on the renewal date at no cost.
