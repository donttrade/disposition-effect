The free tools

The free tools (trade stats, the challenge simulator and the calculators) read your file and your numbers inside your browser tab. Those pages block outgoing requests, so your file is not uploaded and we never see it.

{%- if flags.paid_report %}

The paid habit report

Your browser sends our server only a stripped list of numbers: open and close times, buy or sell, relative size, the realised result and a random symbol number. It contains no name, account number or ticker. The server runs on Cloudflare and may be outside Canada. It uses the numbers to build your report, sends the report back, and does not keep the numbers. Request logging is off.

What the server keeps:

  • a purchase record under a scrambled code: the product, the number of analyses used, the dates and the refund status, without your name or email;
  • scrambled fingerprints of the trades in your first report, to check that a re-test is on the same history;
  • hourly use counters tied to your key and to a scrambled version of your IP address, to limit abuse;
  • if you agree (the box starts unticked), a bare count of the firm name you enter, not linked to you.

The report analyses your trading habits, which may count as profiling. It runs only when you start an analysis with your own file. The results are shown to you only: they are not used for advertising, not compared with other people, and not used to make any decision about you.

Forms, email and payments

  • Forms are sent through Web3Forms, which emails each submission to us; it is hosted on AWS and keeps a 30-day history. The firm and partner forms ask for a work email and firm (required), and your role and what you want measured (optional). The trader waitlist asks for an email, the founding price you would use, and an unticked launch-email consent; the platform you use is optional. No phone numbers are collected.
