What you buy
The {{ r.name }}: a habit test on your own past trades, from a trade file you choose. It measures and describes up to five habits, with the dollar totals of the matching trades, and tests up to two of them against chance in this version. Each checked habit gets one of three results: {{ verdict.steps.none }}, {{ verdict.steps.some }} or {{ verdict.steps.clear }}. On histories with no real habit, a {{ verdict.steps.clear }} on any habit turns up at most {{ verdict.clear_per_100 }} in 100 times across your test and its re-test together, and {{ verdict.steps.some }} or {{ verdict.steps.clear }} at most {{ verdict.some_per_100 }} in 100 times in one result. It includes one re-test 30 to 60 days after your first result. It describes what happened; it does not tell you what to trade.
Technical requirements
A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Files: MetaTrader 5 or 4 HTML report, Interactive Brokers Flex Query CSV, Tradovate Orders CSV or TradingView Order History CSV (all four in beta until real exports confirm the columns), or the donttrade CSV template. Your browser reads the file. {{ strings.p5.long }}

Priced in US dollars. Stripe’s payment page may offer to charge you in your own currency instead, at Stripe’s exchange rate, which includes a 2–4% conversion fee. If you pay in US dollars, your card issuer may charge a currency conversion fee. donttrade receives neither fee.
Payment
Card, through Stripe's checkout page.
When you get it
Right after payment, in your browser. One re-test is included, to run 30 to 60 days after your first result. Your re-test runs on the version of the test that is live when you run it; every change is dated on the corrections page. Access lasts 90 days after purchase, or until 75 days after your first result if that is later.
Restrictions
Two runs: the test and one re-test of the same account (the re-test file has to share at least half of the first file's trades). At most 20,000 rows per file. A habit with fewer than {{ verdict.min_group }} trades in either group it compares is shown as “{{ verdict.steps.too_few }}”. If 5% or more of a file's rows open at exactly 00:00 and are held a whole number of days, as in exports with dates but no times, no habit is tested, and the habits that depend on the order of trades within a day (re-entering soon after a loss, size after a loss, trading after a losing run) get no figure.
