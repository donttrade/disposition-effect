Partners

A partner shares the donttrade test with their audience. People who use the partner’s code get {{ pt.discount_percent }}% off, and the partner earns a commission on the sale.

The deal

  • For the buyer: {{ pt.discount_percent }}% off the price of the donttrade test, with the partner’s code.
  • For the partner: {{ pt.commission_percent }}% of the amount actually paid on each sale made with the partner’s code, on orders placed on or before {{ pt.commission_high_until|longdate }} (Eastern Time); {{ pt.commission_percent_after }}% after that. One time per sale.
  • When it is paid: {{ pt.payout_wait_days }} days after the order, once the refund window has passed. The minimum payout is {{ pt.minimum_payout_usd|usd }}; smaller amounts carry forward.
  • When it is cancelled: a refund or a lost payment dispute cancels the commission on that order. If it was already paid, it is taken off the next payout.
  • Free tests do not earn a commission, and neither does a partner’s own purchase.

Rules for partners

  • Partners are 18 or older. The partner agreement is signed by the seller named in the terms.
  • Say plainly that you earn a commission, in the post or message itself and close to the mention, for example “I earn a commission if you buy.” A code on its own is not a disclosure.
  • Make no claims about trading results or earnings, and do not suggest that a result is typical.
  • Your pay never depends on what you say about donttrade, good or bad.
  • Do not link brokers or prop firms to a code. Do not post a code on coupon sites; we may switch off a code that appears there or breaks these rules.
  • What a partner writes is never shown on donttrade.ca as a testimonial.

Free tests

donttrade sometimes gives a free test to a few people or groups. It is always disclosed as free, asks for nothing in return and is not a request for a review. If you write or post about it, say you received it free.
