Why a flag needs a second check

In a busy market, many unrelated accounts trade the same symbol, the same way, within seconds of each other. On a crowded day there are more of them. A rule that counts shared trades will fire on some of these accounts by chance.

So before you deny a payout, one question matters: does this flag beat coincidence? Flag Check answers that question for one case, and only that question.

We did not raise the flag. We are paid the same whatever the verdict.

{#- 1b. The method in plain words. Matching, firing rule and order test: coincidence-ANALYSIS-PLAN.md 5.1-5.5. The verdict rule is the one in the report template (brain/flagcheck/REPORT-TEMPLATE.md) and flagcheck.mjs. #}

How the check works

  1. Match the trades. A trade of the flagged account and a trade of a linked account match when they are in the same symbol, within 30 seconds of each other. For copy trading they are on the same side. For hedging they are on opposite sides, with sizes within 20% of each other. Each trade is matched once at most.
  2. Apply the flag rule. The rule fires when there are at least 5 matches and they cover at least half of the smaller account’s trades that day.
  3. Test the order. Among the matched trades, who traded first? Coincidence gives about half each way. Copying gives a steady leader. The test needs at least 8 matches that are not tied to the millisecond.
  4. Compare with coincidence. We run the same rule on pairs of accounts from your book with no known link, on the same days. If unlinked pairs match as closely as the flagged pair, the flag does not beat coincidence.

The matching windows, the flag rule and the order test were written down on 7 Nov 2026, before any case. The verdict rule is set out below. They are the same for every firm, and they are not changed after a result.

The exact rules
  • Trades: taker fills only. Fills of one account in one symbol, on one side, at the same timestamp count as one trade.
  • Matching: one-to-one and greedy, earliest pairs first. Copy rule: same symbol, same side, at most 30 seconds apart. Hedge rule: same symbol, opposite sides, at most 30 seconds apart, smaller size at least 80% of the larger.
  • Flag rule: at least 5 matches, and matches divided by the smaller account’s trade count at least 0.5, on the same UTC day. A pair’s match share is its highest such ratio on any day with at least 5 matches.
  • Order test: on the days the rule fires, ties dropped; a two-sided exact binomial test of how often the flagged account went first, against one half. “Ordered” if p is below 0.01. Run only with 8 or more untied matches.
  • Coincidence comparison: every unlinked pair that trades a common symbol on the same days gets the same match share. Rare: counting the flagged pair itself, at most 1% of pairs match at least as closely (split evenly when several linked accounts are tested). Common: more than 5% of the unlinked pairs match at least as closely.
  • Verdict: “Supported” if the rule fires, the match is rare among unlinked pairs, and (for copy trading) the order test is ordered. “Not supported” if the rule does not fire, or the match is common among unlinked pairs. “Cannot tell” in every other case.

For your compliance team: the paid tests

Who will we contract with?

One person. {% include "partials/contracting_party.html" %} We provide whatever identity checks your due diligence needs. The pen name is how the research is published, not a contract term.

Does any account data leave our systems?

No. Your staff run the script inside your systems; we receive only what it prints. We never need access to your systems.

What if the number is bad for us?

Then that is the number. In the free study you read the output first. If you send it, we publish it, named or anonymised as you choose, whatever it shows. In paid work you decide whether anything is published, and the fee is unchanged.

Can you publish without our consent?

No. In the free study we publish only the printed text you send us, and we do not name you without your written agreement. In paid work you review anything describing your data first and may decline publication outright.

Isn’t this a kind of audit?

No. It is a measurement with its limits stated. Please don’t describe it as audited, certified or verified.

What if we stop?

No cancellation fee. {{ pt.kickoff(pilot, "") }} The monthly plan ends at the end of the calendar month in which either side gives notice by email; that month is invoiced as usual.

How do we pay?

Invoiced monthly in USD, in arrears, due within {{ prices.rules.plan_payment_days }} days; bank transfer, or in Canada the CAD equivalent by Interac e-Transfer. {% include "partials/card_timing.html" %}

Can we talk on a call?

We work by email only, so every answer is in writing and can go straight to your compliance file.

Why is there no client list?

No firm has run the free study yet, and there are no paying clients. A firm that publishes its result under its name will be listed, with its consent.

Can we test it first?
