What the results mean

Each tested habit gets one of three results: “distinguishable from noise” (unlikely to be luck), “not distinguishable from noise” (could be luck), or “not enough trades to tell” when either of the two groups it compares has fewer than 30 trades. Each test compares trades from the same days with each other. Dollar figures are historical totals from your file, such as “trades matching this pattern totalled −$X”. They describe what happened; they do not show that a habit caused a result.

Questions

Is this investment advice?

No. It describes your own past trades. It does not recommend any security, trade, size or exit, and past results do not predict future results.

What does the re-test do?

It runs the same report on a newer export of the same account, 30 to 60 days after your first report. Your re-test runs on the version of the report that is live when you run it; every change is dated on the corrections page.
