  • Portfolio-driven disposition effect: the headline did not survive a stricter test (corrected 17 Oct 2026)

    Chinese A-share social-trading portfolios, 2016–18

    A pre-registered replication of An et al. (2024) on the same CC0 panel. Its headline, that the preference for selling winners was about twice as strong when the rest of the portfolio was down (ratio 2.041, now corrected), is not supported: comparing each trader with themselves at the same holding day, the ratio is 1.03 (95% CI 0.95–1.13). Correction logged.

No cookies. What this site stores and sends

This site sets no cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie. It stores your light/dark choice in your own browser and, if you buy the donttrade test, your access key there too. For that test your browser sends our server only stripped trade numbers. The server does not keep them: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first result, to match a re-test to the same history. Your file itself is not uploaded. Card payments are handled by Stripe. Forms send what you type to Web3Forms, which emails it to us, and subscribing opens Substack; their privacy policies apply.
