donttrade measures your own past trades. It is not investment advice, does not recommend any security, trade, size or exit, and is not registered as an adviser. Past results do not predict future results.

No cookies. What this site stores and sends

This site sets no cookies. Visits are counted with Cloudflare Web Analytics, which sets no cookie. It stores your light/dark choice in your own browser and, if you buy the habit report, your access key there too. For that report your browser sends our server only stripped trade numbers. The server does not keep them: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first report, to match a re-test to the same history. Your file itself is not uploaded. Card payments are handled by Stripe. Forms send what you type to Web3Forms, which emails it to us, and subscribing opens Substack; their privacy policies apply.
