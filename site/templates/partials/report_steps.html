  1. 1 Export your full history

    MetaTrader, IBKR, Tradovate and TradingView files are all in beta.

    MetaTrader 5

    History tab, right-click, choose All History, then Report and save it as HTML.

    MetaTrader 4

    Account History tab, right-click, choose All History, then Save as Detailed Report.

    IBKR

    A Flex Query with the Trades section at execution level, as CSV. IBKR allows up to 365 days per export, so pick the last 365 days.

    Tradovate

    The Orders export as CSV, with the date range set to cover all your trades.

    TradingView

    Trading Panel → History → ⋯ → Export data. It saves your order history as CSV.

  2. 2 Drop your files in

    Your browser removes names, account numbers and symbols before anything is sent.

  3. 3 Read your result

    Up to five habits, in this tab. One free re-test in 30 to 60 days.
