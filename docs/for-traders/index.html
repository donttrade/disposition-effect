A habit report built from your own trade file.

It measures and describes up to five habits in your own past trades, like opening a new trade soon after a loss, with the dollar totals of the matching trades, and tests up to two of these habits against chance in this version. It includes one re-test 30 to 60 days after your first report. It doesn’t tell you what to trade.

$39 founding price for the first 100 buyers. One time, in US dollars, with one free re-test 30 to 60 days after your first report. A full refund if you ask within 14 days of buying.

Not investment advice. Before you pay

What it measures

Five habits, measured in your own closed trades as far as your file allows.

HabitThe question about your own trades
Hold gapDid you hold losing trades longer than winning ones? (described, not tested in this version)
Re-entry after a lossDid trades opened within 15 minutes of a loss turn out differently from the rest?
Size after a lossWas the trade after a loss larger than the trade after a win? (described, not tested in this version)
Trading after a losing runDid trades after two or more losses in a row turn out differently?
Unusually large lossesHow many losses were more than twice your typical loss, and what share of your losses they made up (described, not tested)

Each tested habit gets one of three results: “distinguishable from noise”, “not distinguishable from noise”, or “not enough trades to tell” when either of the two groups it compares has fewer than 30 trades. Each test compares trades from the same days with each other. Dollar figures are historical totals from your file, such as “trades matching this pattern totalled −$X”. They describe what happened; they do not show that a habit caused a result.

Your file, and what is sent

  • Files: the MetaTrader 5 or 4 history report, an Interactive Brokers Flex Query, a Tradovate orders export (all three in beta until a real export confirms the columns), or the donttrade CSV template.
  • The free tools open your file in your browser. Those pages block outgoing requests, so the file is not uploaded.
  • For the paid report: Your browser sends our server only stripped trade numbers: open and close times, buy or sell, relative size, the realised result, a random symbol number and a scrambled fingerprint of each row. No name, account number or ticker is sent. The numbers are not kept; we keep only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first report, so a re-test can be matched to the same history, and we delete it on the dates in our privacy notice. Privacy notice.

What’s free, what’s paid, and why

Free tools: $0

They run on your computer, so serving them costs us almost nothing. No account, no card, no time limit. They show what happened in your trades: totals, streaks and drawdowns, and one disposition figure from four counts you type in.

All free tools

Habit report: $39 founding price for the first 100 buyers

The free disposition calculator works from four counts you type in. The report reads the closed trades in your file: the latest 20,000 rows at most, leaving out trades opened more than about 10 years before your latest trade or held longer than 3,650 days. It measures and describes up to five habits, with the dollar totals of the matching trades, and tests up to two of them against chance in this version, comparing trades from the same days. It includes one re-test 30 to 60 days after your first report. Your re-test runs on the version of the report that is live when you run it; every change is dated on the corrections page. The tests run on our server on stripped trade numbers, and building them honestly is the work you pay for.

Questions

Is this investment advice?

No. It describes your own past trades. It does not recommend any security, trade, size or exit, and past results do not predict future results.

How do I buy it?

On the pricing page: read the “Before you pay” summary, then pay $39 by card on Stripe’s page. The report opens in your browser right after payment.

Can I get a refund?

Yes. A full refund if you ask by email within 14 days of buying, even if you have already run your report.

What can I use today?

The free trade-stats dashboard reads the same files and shows the core numbers: win rate, profit factor, drawdown and streaks, in your browser.
