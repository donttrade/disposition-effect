The donttrade test (paid)

Only stripped numbers are sent: no name, account number or ticker.

Your browser sends our server only a stripped list of numbers: open and close times, buy or sell, relative size, the realised result, a random symbol number and a scrambled fingerprint of each row. It contains no name, account number or ticker. The server runs on Cloudflare and may be outside Canada. It uses the numbers to build your result, sends the result back, and does not keep the numbers: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first result, as set out below. Request logging is off.

What the server keeps:

  • a purchase record under a scrambled code: the product, the number of analyses used, the dates and the refund status{% if refund_on %}, whether any habit could be tested and the date of your first result{% endif %}, without your name or email;
  • scrambled fingerprints of the trades in your first result, to check that a re-test is on the same history;
  • hourly use counters tied to your key and to a scrambled version of your IP address, to limit abuse;
  • if you agree (the box starts unticked), a bare count of the firm name you enter, not linked to you.

To check that a re-test uses the same trading history as your first result, we keep a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row (a keyed hash: it cannot be turned back into your trades without our secret key, but it is still linked to your purchase). The report analyses your trading habits, which may count as profiling. It runs only when you start an analysis with your own file. The results are shown to you only: they are not used for advertising, not compared with other people, and not used to make any decision about you.
