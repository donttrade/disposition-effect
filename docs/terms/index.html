The monthly plan for firms

$0.50 per trader account a month, $500 minimum, invoiced after each month. Cancel at any month end.
  • What it is: the donttrade test for firms, run each month on your own records by your own staff with the published script. Today it measures one habit, the disposition effect; habits added later are dated on the corrections page.
  • Price: $0.50 per trader account per month, with a minimum of $500 a month. The number of trader accounts for a month is the number on the accounts: line of that month’s clean run of the script.
  • Invoices: monthly in arrears, by email, in USD, due within 15 days of the invoice date.
  • Paying a year upfront: 15% off the monthly plan price, minimum included. The upfront amount is based on an account count agreed in writing, never less than the minimum; a month whose count is higher is invoiced in arrears for the difference, at the same discount. If the plan ends before the year is over, the months used are charged at the price without the discount and the rest is refunded within 10 business days.
  • Delivery: each month’s result within 5 business days of that month’s clean run.
  • Reproduction: if your own re-run of the same published script version on the same export does not reproduce a month’s figure, that month is not charged.
  • Ending the plan: either side may end the monthly plan by email at any time. It ends at the end of the calendar month in which notice is given. That month is invoiced as usual, minimum included. No cancellation fee. If donttrade ends it, only months with a delivered result are invoiced.
  • Price changes: only with 60 days’ written notice; you may end the plan before the change applies.
  • Licence: the commercial licence for the script runs for as long as the plan does.

Counted in business days, from payment and a clean run.

The Pilot

Your first month, one per firm. Its fee counts toward your plan’s invoices.

Pilot (called “Trial” before 29 Oct 2026).

  • Extract refund: if your extract can’t be made to run with the script, even with our help by email, the fee is refunded in full.
  • Reproduction refund: if your own re-run of the same published script version on the same export (same file hash) does not reproduce the figure, the fee is refunded in full, by the original payment method, within 10 business days.
