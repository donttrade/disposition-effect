What does “tested against chance” mean?

We check whether a habit’s pattern in your trades could just be luck. The report says “distinguishable from noise” when it is unlikely to be luck, and “not distinguishable” when it could be. Up to two habits get this test; the others are described only.

What does the re-test do?

It runs the same report on a newer export of the same account, 30 to 60 days after your first report. {{ RETEST|safe }}

Which files work?
