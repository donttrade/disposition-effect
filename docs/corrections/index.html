Corrections

Corrections are made within 30 days of a confirmed factual error and logged here permanently.

A correction improves the record and costs the author some credibility. That trade is accepted here because the record matters more.

Habit report: method versions

Each version of the habit report’s method is listed here with its date. A report or a re-test runs on the version that is live on the day you run it.

DateMethodWhat it does
23 Oct 20262026-10-23.stats2fFirst version of the habit report. It tests up to two habits against chance: re-entering soon after a loss, and trading after a losing run. It describes the hold gap, size after a loss and unusually large losses, with the dollar totals of the matching trades, without testing them. Rows in your file that share a symbol, a direction and an open minute (for example, a position closed in parts) count as one trade. Rows that open at exactly 00:00 and are held a whole number of days, as in exports with dates but no times, are never combined; if they are 5% or more of a file's rows, nothing in that file is tested and size after a loss is not described.

No cookies. What this site stores and sends

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It stores your light/dark choice in your own browser and, if you buy the habit report, your access key there too. For that report your browser sends our server only stripped trade numbers. The server does not keep them: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first report, to match a re-test to the same history. Your file itself is not uploaded. Card payments are handled by Stripe. Forms send what you type to Web3Forms, which emails it to us, and subscribing opens Substack; their privacy policies apply.
