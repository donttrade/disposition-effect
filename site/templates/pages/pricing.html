Questions about price

Why publish prices?

So you know the cost before you write, and every firm pays the same.

Do you take card payments?

Yes. {% include "partials/card_timing.html" %} Bank transfer, or Interac e-Transfer in Canada, also work.

What is the difference between the {{ pilot.name }}, a Snapshot and a Memo?

The {{ pilot.name }} is your first Snapshot at an introductory price, one per firm. A Snapshot is one figure for one population and period. A Memo adds your ratio set beside the public study’s, with the differences explained in writing.

Is the {{ pilot.name }} fee lost if we go ahead?

No. {{ pilot.credit_rule }} of delivery.

Can the price change after we accept?

No. It is fixed in writing in the scope you accept.

Does paying change the result?

No. Independence policy
