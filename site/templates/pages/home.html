What the results mean

In plain words: the pattern is unlikely to be luck. “Not distinguishable” means it could be luck. Each tested habit gets one of three results: “distinguishable from noise”, “not distinguishable from noise”, or “not enough trades to tell” when either of the two groups it compares has fewer than 30 trades. Each test compares trades from the same days with each other. Dollar figures are historical totals from your file. They describe what happened; they do not show that a habit caused a result.

For firms: how it works

Traders plan how they will handle wins and losses. What they actually do can differ. We call that difference the plan gap, and we measure it on your own records, starting with one measure: are your traders more likely to sell a winning trade than a losing one?

  1. Tell us which traders and which dates. A short form or an email, about five minutes.
  2. Get a written scope and a fixed price within 1 business day. Your staff run the public script on your own systems. Only totals come back.
  3. Get the number, how sure it is, and a one-page memo. You decide whether anything is published.

Questions

{%- if flags.ask_box %} {{ answer_box("pg") }} {%- endif %}
Is this advice?

No. It describes past trades and past behaviour. It does not tell anyone what to trade, how much, or when to exit.

Where does my trade file go?

The free tools read it inside your browser tab; those pages block every outgoing request, so the file is not uploaded. When the habit report launches, the page that runs it will say exactly what it sends.

What do the free tools cost?

Nothing, with no account and no card. They run in your browser, so serving them costs us almost nothing.

Is our firm’s data safe?

Your trading records never leave your systems. Your staff run our public script on your own computers, and you can read every line first. Only the totals it prints come back to us: no account IDs, no trades, no names.

What if no effect is found?

Then that is your answer, and it is a finished result. The price is fixed before anyone sees data, so you pay the same either way. The result stays private unless you choose to publish it.

What does a firm engagement cost?

Every price is published on the pricing page, the same for every firm, and fixed in writing in the scope before any data is seen. No cancellation fees.

Refunds?

Firms: a Pilot is refunded in full if your extract can’t be made to run, or if your own re-run of the same script on the same export does not reproduce the figure. Traders: nothing is sold yet. Cancellation and refund terms
