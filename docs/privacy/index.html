The free tools

The free tools (trade stats, the challenge simulator and the calculators) read your file and your numbers inside your browser tab. Those pages block outgoing requests, so your file is not uploaded and we never see it.

The paid habit report

Your browser sends our server only a stripped list of numbers: open and close times, buy or sell, relative size, the realised result, a random symbol number and a scrambled fingerprint of each row. It contains no name, account number or ticker. The server runs on Cloudflare and may be outside Canada. It uses the numbers to build your report, sends the report back, and does not keep the numbers: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first report, as described below. Request logging is off.

What the server keeps:

  • a purchase record under a scrambled code: the product, the number of analyses used, the dates and the refund status, without your name or email;
  • scrambled fingerprints of the trades in your first report, to check that a re-test is on the same history;
  • hourly use counters tied to your key and to a scrambled version of your IP address, to limit abuse;
  • if you agree (the box starts unticked), a bare count of the firm name you enter, not linked to you.

To check that a re-test uses the same trading history as your first report, we keep a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row (a keyed hash: it cannot be turned back into your trades without our secret key, but it is still linked to your purchase). We delete these fingerprints as soon as your included re-test has run, or 75 days after your first report, whichever comes first. If you have an active Habit Tracker, we keep them while it is active and delete them 15 days after it ends.

The report analyses your trading habits, which may count as profiling. It runs only when you start an analysis with your own file. The results are shown to you only: they are not used for advertising, not compared with other people, and not used to make any decision about you.

Forms, email and payments

  • Forms are sent through Web3Forms, which emails each submission to us; it is hosted on AWS and keeps a 30-day history. The firm and partner forms ask for a work email and firm (required), and your role and what you want measured (optional). No phone numbers are collected.
  • Email is handled by Gmail (Google). We keep form emails there.
  • The newsletter runs on Substack; see Substack’s privacy policy.
  • Card payments (the firm Pilot, and the habit report) are handled by Stripe; donttrade never sees full card numbers. For payment data Stripe acts as an independent controller; see Stripe’s privacy policy.
  • If you buy the habit report: Stripe shows us the name and email address you enter at checkout. We use them only to send you the copy of your agreement, to answer your emails (a refund, a new access link) and to deal with a payment dispute. If you dispute the payment with your card issuer, we may give Stripe the dates of your purchase and of your analyses, and the number of analyses used, as evidence. Our emails with you stay in our Gmail inbox, and a private list of sales (the checkout code, the date, your name and email, and the dates we sent the copy of your agreement or a refund) is kept on our own computer, not on our server. Both are kept for the same period as purchase records (below).
  • Web3Forms, Google, GitHub, Stripe, Substack and Cloudflare are mostly in the United States, so what you send may be stored or processed outside Canada, and the authorities there may access it under their laws.

What we do with what you send

  • We use it to reply, scope the work and invoice.
  • Enquiries that don’t lead to an engagement are deleted within 12 months.
  • Engagement and purchase records are kept for as long as Canadian tax law requires (six years).
  • Addresses given before launch for the one launch email are used for that email only, then deleted unless the person buys.
  • Nothing is sold. We use these providers only to run the site, email, the newsletter, forms and card payments.

Client data

For firm engagements we never receive account records, only the aggregate counts the script prints.

Your rights

You can ask by email to see, correct or delete your information, or for a copy in a common format. We reply within 30 days. You can also complain to the Office of the Privacy Commissioner of Canada, in Quebec to the Commission d’accès à l’information, in the EU to your country’s data protection authority, and in the UK to the Information Commissioner’s Office. If a privacy breach creates a real risk of significant harm to you, we tell you.
