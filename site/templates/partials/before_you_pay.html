What you buy
{{ r.name }}: a report on habits in your own past trades, built from a trade file you choose. It measures and describes up to five habits, with the dollar totals of the matching trades, and tests up to two of them against chance in this version. It includes one re-test 30 to 60 days after your first report. It describes what happened; it does not tell you what to trade.
Technical requirements
A current version of Chrome, Edge, Firefox or Safari. Files: MetaTrader 5 or 4 HTML report, Interactive Brokers Flex Query CSV or Tradovate Orders CSV (all three in beta until a real export confirms the columns), or the donttrade CSV template. Your browser reads the file. {{ strings.p5.long }}

When you get it
Right after payment, in your browser. One re-test is included, to run 30 to 60 days after your first report. Your re-test runs on the version of the report that is live when you run it; every change is dated on the corrections page. Access lasts 90 days after purchase, or until 75 days after your first report if that is later.
Restrictions
Two runs: the report and one re-test of the same account (the re-test file has to share at least half of the first file's trades). At most 20,000 rows per file. A tested habit with fewer than 30 trades in either group is shown as "not enough trades to tell". If 5% or more of a file's rows open at exactly 00:00 and are held a whole number of days, as in exports with dates but no times, no habit is tested, and the habits that depend on the order of trades within a day (re-entering soon after a loss, size after a loss, trading after a losing run) are not described.
