Measure it in your own trades

These free tools read your trade file in your browser. The file is not uploaded.

  • Trade statsWin rate, profit factor, longest streaks and drawdown from your file.
  • Drawdown and recovery calculatorThe depth and length of each of your drawdowns.
  • Challenge Readiness CheckYour past days replayed under a prop firm’s published rules.

How these pages are made

  • Every table is computed by a script in the public repository when the site is built, and the same table is offered as a CSV. The site’s tests recompute each value a second way.
  • A paper is cited only after its title, journal and year were confirmed with Crossref; the date is on the page.
  • These pages describe arithmetic and measurement. They are not investment advice and say nothing about any trade, size or exit. A mistake is logged on Corrections.
  • The scripts and data behind the studies are listed on Methods and code.
