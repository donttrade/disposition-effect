How payment works today

  • Every firm engagement is invoiced by email, in USD. International clients pay by bank transfer; clients in Canada may pay the CAD equivalent by Interac e-Transfer, at the Bank of Canada daily rate on the invoice date. Transfer and bank fees are paid by the sender.
  • The engagement agreement is sent with the scope, before you pay. A blank template is available on request.
  • Card payments are accepted through a secure Stripe link, sent only after you accept the scope.
  • We never ask for card details by email.

Cancellations, refunds and the Pilot

  • Firms: no cancellation fees. The Pilot is refundable in full until your staff first run the script on your records (data kick-off), and after that if your extract can’t be made to run, or through the reproduction refund. Snapshot, Memo and Outcomes pack are paid 50% on acceptance and 50% within 7 days of delivery; if either side stops, completed work is billed pro rata.
  • Traders: nothing is sold yet. When the report launches, a full refund is available if you ask within 14 days of buying.

Questions about price

Why publish prices?

So you know the cost before you write, and every firm pays the same.

Do you take card payments?

Yes. Card payments are accepted through a secure Stripe link, sent only after you accept the scope. Bank transfer, or Interac e-Transfer in Canada, also work.

What is the difference between the Pilot, a Snapshot and a Memo?

The Pilot is your first Snapshot at an introductory price, one per firm. A Snapshot is one figure for one population and period. A Memo adds your ratio set beside the public study’s, with the differences explained in writing.

Is the Pilot fee lost if we go ahead?

No. Credited in full toward a Memo signed within 60 days of delivery.

Can the price change after we accept?

No. It is fixed in writing in the scope you accept.

Does paying change the result?

No. Independence policy
