What does “tested against chance” mean?

We check whether a habit’s pattern in your trades could just be luck. Your result says “distinguishable from noise” when it is unlikely to be luck, and “not distinguishable” when it could be. Up to two habits get this test; the others are described only.

What does the re-test do?

It runs the same test on a newer export of the same account, 30 to 60 days after your first result. {{ RETEST|safe }}

Which files work?

MetaTrader 5 or 4, IBKR, Tradovate and TradingView exports (all in beta), or the donttrade CSV template.

Where does my file go?

It stays in your browser. {{ strings.p5.long }}

No. It describes your own past trades. It does not tell you what to trade, how much, or when to exit.
