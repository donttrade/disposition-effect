What the report measures

Five habits, each measured in your own closed trades.

HabitThe question about your own trades
Hold gapDid you hold losing trades longer than winning ones?
Re-entry after a lossDid trades opened within 15 minutes of a loss turn out differently from the rest?
Size after a lossWas the trade after a loss larger than the trade after a win?
Trading after a losing runDid trades after two or more losses in a row turn out differently?
Unusually large lossesHow many losses were more than twice your typical loss, and what share of your losses they made up (described, not tested)

Each tested habit gets one of three results: “distinguishable from noise”, “not distinguishable from noise”, or “not enough trades to tell” when either of the two groups it compares has fewer than 30 trades. Each test compares trades from the same days with each other. Dollar figures are historical totals from your file, such as “trades matching this pattern totalled −$X”. They describe what happened; they do not show that a habit caused a result.

How it works

  1. Export your history. A MetaTrader 5 or 4 report, an IBKR Flex Query or a Tradovate orders export (all three in beta), or the donttrade CSV template.
  2. Your browser reads it. It removes names, account numbers and symbols, and sends our server only stripped trade numbers, which are not stored.
  3. Read your report. Run the free re-test on the same account 30 to 60 days after your first report.

Questions

Is our firm’s data safe?

Your trading records never leave your systems. Your staff run our public script on your own computers, and you can read every line first. Only the totals it prints come back to us: no account IDs, no trades, no names.

What if no effect is found?

Then that is your answer, and it is a finished result. The price is fixed before anyone sees data, so you pay the same either way. The result stays private unless you choose to publish it.

What does it cost?

Every price is published on the pricing page, the same for every firm, and fixed in writing in the scope before any data is seen. No cancellation fees.

Is this advice?

No. It describes past behaviour. It does not tell anyone what to trade.

Where does my trade file go?

The free tools read it inside your browser tab; those pages block every outgoing request, so the file is not uploaded. When the habit report launches, the page that runs it will say exactly what it sends.
