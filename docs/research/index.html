Research

Every study below names the data it used and links its script, or says when the script is still to be published. Published whether or not the result was interesting.

New here? Start with the disposition-effect study, then the four negative results.

FEATURED · 6 OCT 2026

76.1%

About 3 in 4 traders realised gains more readily than losses: 3,547 of 4,659. Public data: Chinese A-share social-trading portfolios, 2016–18.

Open the chart full size (SVG, new tab) ↗

Per trader then averaged: ratio 1.61 (chart). Pooled across every observation: ratio 1.47 (PGR 0.0785, PLR 0.0535).

Update, 17 Oct 2026: the direction survives when holding age is held fixed; the size depends on how “gain” is timed, and a V-shaped selling pattern is not ruled out. Robustness update →

Chinese A-shares, Xueqiu social-trading portfolios, Jun 2016 – Mar 2018 · CC0 panel, Jin, Li & Zhu (2021) · reproduce.py · published 6 Oct 2026 · reviewed 17 Oct 2026

The insider-buying edge was mostly 2008.

Forty years of research said insiders beat the market. In 470,754 SEC Form 4 records, take out 2008–09 and an average 21-day edge above 0.62% is ruled out.

  • 76.1% of 4,659 retail traders realised gains more readily than losses

    Chinese A-share social-trading portfolios, 2016–18

    A PGR/PLR replication of Odean (1998) on free CC0 data — 4,731 traders, 2,780,879 trader-stock-lot-days — and the three caveats that move the result. Robustness update, 17 Oct 2026: the direction survives a holding-age control; the size depends on how “gain” is timed, and a V-shaped selling pattern is not ruled out.

    replication

    Data: CC0 panel, 24 Jun 2016 – 27 Mar 2018 · Script: reproduce.py · Published 6 Oct 2026 · Reviewed 17 Oct 2026

  • The insider-buying edge was mostly 2008

    Forty years of research said insiders beat the market. In 470,754 SEC Form 4 records, 2006–2026, two years supplied 94% of the excess return of the era that looked like it worked. Take them out and nothing is left.

    negative resultinsider filings

    Data: SEC Form 4 bulk files, 81 quarters, 2006 – 2026 Q1 · Script: form4/ · Published 11 Oct 2026 · Reviewed 17 Oct 2026

  • Portfolio-driven disposition effect: the headline did not survive a stricter test (corrected 17 Oct 2026)

    Chinese A-share social-trading portfolios, 2016–18

    A pre-registered replication of An et al. (2024) on the same CC0 panel. Its headline, that the preference for selling winners was about twice as strong when the rest of the portfolio was down (ratio 2.041, now corrected), is not supported: comparing each trader with themselves at the same holding day, the ratio is 1.03 (95% CI 0.95–1.13). Correction logged.

    correctedpre-registeredreplication

    Data: CC0 panel, 24 Jun 2016 – 27 Mar 2018 · Script: pdde/ · Pre-registered 9 Oct 2026 · Published 16 Oct 2026 · Corrected 17 Oct 2026

  • A pre-registered test of volatility-scaled momentum failed its own kill criteria at five positions

    The mechanism passed on 45.7 years of factor data (Sharpe 0.78, t 5.25); the five-position version with costs did not, and no money was committed. A negative research result, not a strategy or a signal.

  • We tested 26 swing-trading strategies under pre-registered rules. None survived.

    Three monthly ETF rules and 23 daily swing rules on 22 ETFs, each fingerprinted with a hash before any result was computed and judged once on a locked holdout, net of a 0.20% round-trip cost. None passed; 0 of 32 across the project. A negative research result, not a strategy or a signal.

    negative resultpre-registered

    Data: free vendor-adjusted daily ETF prices, snapshot 16 Oct 2026 · Code: to be published with this study · Pre-registered and published 16 Oct 2026

  • We tested 50 trading strategies, from swing trades to day trades. None survived.

    The swing study’s 32 strategies plus 18 new rules: 7 investing, 7 overnight and 4 day-trading, each fingerprinted with a hash before any result was computed. 0 of 50 across the project; 0 of 44 under hashed pre-registration. The opening-range breakout on QQQ made +3.56 bp per trade before costs and lost it to 5.47 bp of costs. A negative research result, not a strategy or a signal.

donttrade measures your own past trades. It is not investment advice, does not recommend any security, trade, size or exit, and is not registered as an adviser. Past results do not predict future results.

This site sets no cookies and runs no analytics. It stores your light/dark choice in your own browser and, if you buy the habit report, your access key there too. For that report your browser sends our server only stripped trade numbers. The server does not keep them: of those numbers it keeps only a scrambled fingerprint of each trade row from your first report, to match a re-test to the same history. Your file itself is not uploaded. Card payments are handled by Stripe. Forms send what you type to Web3Forms, which emails it to us, and subscribing opens Substack; their privacy policies apply.
