{#- 5. Copy-leader (existing id). #}

Copy-trading platforms: the copy-leader persistence test

Sold to platforms only, never to followers. For copy-trading apps, social trading networks, crypto exchanges with copy features and vault front-ends.

Does your leader ranking predict what followers actually earn after copying costs and delay? The test answers that out of sample: leaders are ranked on one period and judged on a later period the ranking never saw. It is adjusted for luck, so a ranking counts only if it does better than chance would.

  • A sealed method. The ranking rule, the periods, the cost and delay assumptions and the pass mark are fixed in writing before anyone sees data, and they don’t change afterwards.
  • Run on your systems. Your engineers run a script we send with the scope, and can read every line of it first. Only aggregate results come back. We hold no follower, leader or account record.
  • About your ranking, not about people. The result names no leader and scores no one.
  • Not advice. It does not recommend any trader or leader, to you or to your followers, and makes no promise about future results. It is not an audit: please don’t call any leader or ranking audited, certified or verified.

Which engagement: the {{ pilot.name }} ({{ pilot.price_usd|usd }} USD) covers one ranking rule and one period, one per platform; a Snapshot ({{ products.snapshot.price_usd|usd }}) the same; a Memo ({{ products.memo.price_usd|usd }}) every rule and period agreed in the scope; an Outcomes pack ({{ products.outcomes.price_usd|usd }}) adds a re-check on a later period. Payment, delivery and refunds are those of the engagement.

{#- 6. Claims test (existing id). #}

Firms that make behaviour claims: the claims substantiation test

We report the result whether it supports the claim or not. For trading journals, education sellers, prop firms and brokers whose marketing says their product changes how traders behave, such as “our journal makes you more disciplined”.

Before anyone sees data, the claim is written down as a test: which behaviour, which users, which periods, what comparison, and what result would support it. That test is sealed (fingerprinted with a SHA-256 hash) and then run once, as written.

  • Run on your systems. Your engineers run a script we send with the scope; only aggregate results come back.
  • Either answer is a finished result. The fee is the same either way, and you decide whether anything is published.
  • Limits stated. We tell you in the scope, before you pay, whether your records can test the claim at all. A before-and-after comparison, for example, cannot by itself show that your product caused a change.
  • A test, with its result and its limits. It is not an audit. Please don’t call the claim or the product certified, verified or audited.

The law, in neutral terms (not legal advice). In Canada, the Competition Act treats as reviewable conduct a representation to the public, made to promote a product or a business interest, in the form of “a statement, warranty or guarantee of the performance, efficacy or length of life of a product that is not based on an adequate and proper test thereof, the proof of which lies on the person making the representation” (s. 74.01(1)(b)). Whether a particular test is adequate and proper for a particular claim is for you and your legal advisers to judge. We give no legal advice, and we don’t say that our test meets that standard.

{#- 7. What stays on your systems, and what comes back. #}

What stays on your systems

Your trade records and account identifiers. The script runs inside your systems on a five-column extract, one row per open position per day plus a row on the day it closes, with no names. From the export guide:

account_id,date,position_id,closed,pnl
a7f3,2026-03-02,p91,0,110.50
a7f3,2026-03-03,p91,0,-40.25
a7f3,2026-03-04,p91,1,240.00

The first two rows are the unrealised mark at each end of day; the last is the realisation. A list of closed trades alone is refused by the script, by design. To see the shape first, run it on the synthetic file that make_fixture.py writes.
